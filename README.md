What is AeroLab?
It is a program which I started as a student in aerospace engineering at Polytechnic University of Turin. The reason why I invest a lot of time here is to set myself in the environment of the engineering world, to learn not only by studying theoretical concepts, but also by applying them to real engineering problems, to open my eyes and show me where my difficulties are and push me to improve them. AeroLab should not be seen as a simple project, but as part of my development as an aerospace engineer. Every module added to AeroLab must teach me at least one concept in physics, mathematics or programming.

What can it do?
AeroLab develops simulation tools to learn physics, mathematics and aerospace engineering. Inside it has a wide variety of formulas form various fields such as mechanics and thermodynamics, each accompained by its own graphs.
